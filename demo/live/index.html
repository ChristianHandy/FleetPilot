Starting your own FleetPilot

The real FleetPilot runs right here in this tab, with an emulated homelab: Proxmox nodes, a TrueNAS box, a backup server, Linux and Windows machines, a FRITZ!Box, OpenWrt and OPNsense. Click anything: updates, Wake-on-LAN, VMs, fans and router checks all work. Nothing leaves your browser.

Things to try
  1. Hosts: update docker-01 and watch the apt output.
  2. Hosts: lab-01 is off. Wake it with Wake-on-LAN.
  3. Below: Let a disk fail on nas, then open SMART and the Outbox tab.
  4. VMs: start k3s-node, or shut down jellyfin.
  5. Below: publish a FRITZ!OS update, then Routers → FRITZ!Box → Check for updates.
  6. Fans: change a fan's speed and watch RPM and temperature follow.
  7. Vulnerabilities: see real CVEs for docker-01 and its containers, update it, then Rescan.
